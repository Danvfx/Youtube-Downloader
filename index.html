◆ About Us

People can achieve far more than their current roles.

DANCHE Training and Consultancy, Inc. was founded on November 17, 2016 on a simple conviction: employees in every organization carry untapped potential. Our work is to unlock it — through tailored workshops, consultancy, and coaching that turn proven HR and leadership practices into skills people use the very next day.

We serve both government agencies and private companies, designing each engagement around the realities of your workplace rather than one-size-fits-all theory.

Our Mission

To inform, educate, assist, and guide individuals who are uncertain about their career paths — empowering their personal and professional growth.

Our Vision

Workplaces where every employee is developed beyond their current role, led by leaders who inspire confidence, clarity, and growth.

Our Approach

Practical over theoretical. Every program is built from real consulting experience with 100+ organizations and focuses on immediately applicable tools.
