Communication Plan

How we stay in sync

  • Weekly Google Meet sync every Monday to review progress and blockers.
  • Shared Google Doc for meeting notes, always updated live during calls.
  • Task status tracked in a shared Google Sheet, updated after every work session.
  • Deadlines and milestones posted to a shared Google Calendar visible to all members.
  • Feedback on drafts left as comments directly in Docs and Slides.
  • Final files organized in a single shared Drive folder with clear naming.
