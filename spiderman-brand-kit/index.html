Week 4

Publish

Last pass on the copy, then the site went live.

Ground rules

What we agreed on in week one

  • Monday call at 7pm. Thirty minutes, no exceptions.
  • Notes go into the doc during the call, not after it.
  • Update your row in the tracker the moment you finish something.
  • Deadlines go on the shared calendar the day we agree on them.
  • Feedback goes in file comments, never in chat.
  • File names follow the pattern written in the Drive README.
